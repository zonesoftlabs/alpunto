About Al Punto

Al Punto is a digital technology news project focused on delivering concise and accessible updates about technology, artificial intelligence, software, devices and the digital world.

Our goal is simple: transform relevant technology news into short, easy-to-understand video content designed for social media.

How it works

Al Punto uses an automated content production workflow to assist with the preparation and distribution of short-form technology news videos.

Content is prepared for publication across supported social media platforms through their official publishing tools and APIs.

Where we publish

Al Punto distributes short-form technology news through social media platforms.

YouTube
Instagram
TikTok

Our approach

We focus on short, informative content that makes technology news easier to discover and understand.
