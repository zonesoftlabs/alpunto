Privacy Policy

Al Punto

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Overview

Al Punto is a digital technology news project that prepares and distributes short-form technology news content through supported social media platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when Al Punto uses integrations with third-party platforms and their official APIs.

2. Information processed through platform integrations

When an authorized social media account is connected to Al Punto, the integration may process information made available by that platform and permitted by the account owner, such as:

  • Account identifiers and basic profile information.
  • Authorization credentials required to access the platform API.
  • Permissions granted by the account owner.
  • Information related to content submitted for publication.
  • Publication identifiers, status information and links returned by the platform.

3. How information is used

Information obtained through platform integrations is used only as necessary to operate Al Punto's publishing workflow, including:

  • Authenticating an authorized account.
  • Submitting content for publication.
  • Checking publication processing and status.
  • Confirming successful publication.
  • Maintaining operational records needed to prevent duplicate publications and diagnose errors.

4. Access credentials

Access tokens and other credentials used to communicate with third-party APIs are treated as confidential configuration data. They are not intended to be included in public content or public operational logs.

5. Data sharing

Al Punto does not sell personal information. Information is shared with third-party platforms only as required to provide the requested publishing functionality and communicate with their official APIs.

6. Third-party platforms

Al Punto may integrate with services such as TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. Use of those services is also subject to the privacy policies, terms and developer requirements of the respective platform.

7. Data retention and deletion

Operational information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot the publishing workflow. Temporary media files used solely to facilitate publication may be deleted after they are no longer required.

An account owner may revoke Al Punto's access through the applicable third-party platform. Requests concerning data associated with an Al Punto integration may also be submitted using the contact information below.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect credentials and operational information from unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Al Punto's services, integrations or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

Questions, privacy inquiries or deletion requests regarding Al Punto can be directed to the operator of the project through the contact channel published on the Al Punto website.
